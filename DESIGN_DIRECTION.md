# MengTo comparison direction

- Thesis: a night-market field guide that turns an unfamiliar menu into a clear decision.
- Hero focal point: oversized, compressed promise plus a physical-feeling orange scan control.
- Type: Archivo Black for decisive display moments; Manrope for legible product copy.
- Palette: ink `#171712`, saffron `#ff6b2c`, field paper `#f3efe7`, graphite `#655f55`.
- Layout: mobile field sheet with measured grid, hard rules, and offset print shadows.
- Motion: one restrained entrance and tactile CSS states; reduced-motion resolves immediately.
- Smooth scroll: none. The compact app flow does not benefit from scroll interception.
- Three.js: omitted. Spatial rendering would distract from the camera-to-decision task.
- Assets: no third-party or generated imagery; UI is built from honest product content and interface icons.
